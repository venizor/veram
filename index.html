Do you ship across India?

Yes, we ship to every pin code in India. Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,999 and above; your bag shows how close you are.

Which size should I pick?

Every tee is cut oversized with a drop shoulder. Take your usual size for the relaxed look, or go one size down for a closer fit. Exact measurements are in the fit guide.

Can I exchange if it doesn't fit?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram after delivery with your order details and we'll help you swap sizes, subject to stock.

How do I care for French terry?

Wash cold and inside out, skip the bleach, and dry in the shade. Don't iron directly on the print. Treat it well and it keeps its weight and colour for years.

Will designs be restocked?

Drops are limited runs. If a size sells out, join the Drop 02 list below — waitlist members hear first about anything new.
